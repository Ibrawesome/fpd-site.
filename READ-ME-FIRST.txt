HOW TO PUT THIS SITE ONLINE (simple steps)

Admin login:  IBRAWESOME  /  6793

STEP 1 - Make a GitHub account (free): https://github.com
STEP 2 - Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it: fpd-site. Click "Create repository".
STEP 3 - Click "uploading an existing file".
         Open this folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING inside it
         (index.html, netlify.toml, package.json and the "netlify" folder)
         and drag them into GitHub. Click "Commit changes".
STEP 4 - Go to https://app.netlify.com > "Add new site" > "Import an existing project" > GitHub > pick fpd-site.
         Leave all settings as they are. Click "Deploy".
STEP 5 - Wait 1-2 minutes. Open your Netlify link. Register a test student,
         then log in as admin to see that student.

OPTIONAL (safer): Netlify > Site configuration > Environment variables. Add:
   ADMIN_USER = your own username
   ADMIN_PASS = your own password
   AUTH_SECRET = any long random text
Then Deploys > Trigger deploy.
